# Liner Notes roadmap

## Next up
- Website: what it does, screenshots, download button for the latest release, phone setup guide, Buy Me a Coffee

## Future features
- Stylus hours: track hours played on vinyl and say when the stylus is due for replacing (about 800 to 1,000 hours)
- Shows near you: upcoming concerts for the artist on screen
- More like this: similar artists in the info panel, with Spotify and Bandcamp links
- Your shelf: every record you've played as a collection; Discogs collection sync, marketplace value, records not played in a while
- End-of-side chime on the PC
- On this day: "a year ago today you played..." notes on the between-records screen
- Smart lights that take on the album cover's colors (Philips Hue and similar)
- A public "now playing" link friends can open (needs accounts and hosting)

## Bigger projects
- Mac version (needs a Mac to test on; Apple Developer account to share it)
- Phone apps (Android and iPhone), including photographing a record's liner notes: keep the photo and turn the text into readable text attached to the album and songs
- Accounts: sync history and saved songs across devices
- Code signing to remove the Windows "unknown publisher" warning (on hold)
