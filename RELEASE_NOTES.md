- Recap cards: open your listening history (the chart icon) and tap "Make a shareable recap card". Pick the last 24 hours, a week, a month or a year, and step back with Earlier to see past ones. Each card shows songs played, hours, artists and how much was on vinyl, your top record and the next four, top artists, artists new to you, songs you saved, and your top song.
- On the PC, "Save to Pictures" puts the card in Pictures > Liner Notes. On a phone, "Share" sends it straight to Messages, Instagram and other apps. "Download" works everywhere.
