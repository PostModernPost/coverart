Know who's on the record.

Put a record on. Liner Notes hears it, names the song, and puts the cover, the lyrics and who played what on a screen next to your turntable. Then it follows the record song by song, side by side, and tells you when to flip it. No button to press. It just listens.

Download for Windows

Free. Windows 10 and 11. Windows may say the publisher is unknown: click More info, then Run anyway.

Drop the needle. It does the rest.

  1. It hears the record

    A mic in the room, a line from your phono preamp, or an old phone by the speakers picks up the music. Shazam names the song. Liner Notes only starts listening properly once there's music, and you can turn listening off whenever you like.

  2. It follows the whole side

    One song in, it loads the record's tracklist and moves to the next song as each one ends, even the songs Shazam doesn't know. It knows a quiet break from the end of a song, and when the side runs out it tells you to flip.

  3. It knows which record

    Studio album or greatest hits, a 45 with its B side, a compilation of different artists: it works out the release from the songs it hears. If it can't, you find it or name it in a couple of taps.

  4. It shows the record

    The cover, the song, the side and track, and where you are in it, big enough to read from the couch. Timed lyrics scroll along beside the art.

That old phone in the drawer is the screen.

Plug an Android phone into the PC and it becomes a little display that lives by the turntable, and its microphone can do the listening right next to the speakers. It wakes up for the next record, and goes dark when nothing's playing or your PC sleeps. An iPhone works as the screen over Wi-Fi.

  • Spotify on the PC. Anything playing in a music app on the PC shows up too.

Getting started

  1. Download and run the installer

    No admin password needed. Liner Notes opens in its own window and keeps a record icon by the clock.

  2. Pick what listens

    Settings > Microphone > Listen with. A mic near the speakers works; a cable from your phono preamp to the PC's line-in works best. The meter shows when it hears music.

  3. Put a record on

    The first song tells it what's playing, and it follows from there. Want a screen by the turntable? Settings > Phone screen walks you through an old phone.
