Privacy

Liner Notes has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What it learns about your listening stays on your own PC or phone.

What stays on your devices

Your listening history, saved songs, your shelf, stylus hours and settings are stored on the device running Liner Notes: in Liner Notes' folder on your PC, or inside the Android app when the phone works on its own. Nothing is sent to the developer. There is no Liner Notes server.

The microphone

To recognize the record playing, Liner Notes listens through a microphone: one on your PC, or your phone's. Audio is only used while it listens for music. Short clips, about 12 seconds, go to Shazam to name the song. Nothing is recorded or kept beyond the last clip, which stays in memory so you can play it back in Settings.

The Android app works on its own or with your PC. On its own, the phone sends those short clips to Shazam itself. Connected to your PC, it sends the phone's microphone audio only to Liner Notes on your own PC, over your home Wi-Fi or a USB cable, and only when your PC is set to listen through the phone.

Services Liner Notes looks things up with

To show what's playing, Liner Notes (on your PC, or on the phone when it works on its own) asks these services about the song, using the song's name and artist: Shazam (recognition), Apple Music, Deezer and MusicBrainz (covers and album details), Discogs (tracklists, credits and your collection if you connect it), LRCLIB and lyrics.ovh (lyrics), and Wikipedia and Last.fm (bios). Each has its own privacy policy.

If you connect Spotify, Last.fm or Discogs, Liner Notes uses that connection only for what you turned on (saving songs, scrobbling plays, reading or adding to your collection). The keys stay on the device that connected, and you can disconnect in Settings.

The phone app and your PC

When you connect the Android app to your PC over Wi-Fi, the PC gives the app a key so it can change Liner Notes' settings. The key is stored in the app and on your PC; disconnect the phone in Settings > Phone screen to cancel it.
